# Institution logos: sources

All files were downloaded on 2026-11-17. Raster files were trimmed, had metadata stripped, were resized to at most 600px wide and saved as PNG32 with transparency. All are official marks of Rwandan public bodies, used only to identify them ("names shown for reference, not endorsement"). They are not ours to license. Ask the institution before any other use.
